Current interdisciplinary work

Oil-sands methane & aquatic ecosystems

Since May 2023, I have contributed to an NSERC Alliance Missions project on methane emissions from Alberta oil-sands territories. My work includes modelling hydrocarbon biodegradation and greenhouse-gas emissions and collaborating with Dr. Keith Tierney’s laboratory on mathematical analysis of methane impacts on aquatic systems, including zebrafish physiological data.

Methods

Mechanistic + data-driven modelling

I am especially interested in frameworks that preserve biological mechanism while learning from data: bifurcation analysis, structured population models, stochastic systems, empirical dynamical modelling, S-maps, convergent cross mapping, machine learning, and universal differential equations.
