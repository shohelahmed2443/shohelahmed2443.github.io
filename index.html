About

Mathematics connecting behaviour to biological dynamics.

My doctoral research, Modelling Foraging Behaviour in Ecological Dynamics, studies how behavioural decisions, individual variation, and environmental forcing influence population and ecosystem outcomes. I combine dynamical systems, bifurcation theory, trait-structured models, stochastic processes, empirical dynamical modelling, and data-driven methods.
